The reference that decides a matter rarely ranks first.

Sambyal IP is an IP search and analytics practice for life sciences and HiTech. It works by referral, and takes one engagement at a time.

The position

Retrieval is no longer the hard part. Any competent tool now returns more prior art than a matter can absorb, and the cost of producing results is falling toward zero. What none of it does is decide.

The work that survives that shift is judgment. Scoping a search to the question actually being asked. Recognising the reference that reads on a claim when nothing in its language says so. Producing an analysis that holds when someone is paid to break it.

This practice is built for that end state rather than adapted to it. It does not compete on volume, turnaround tables or headcount, because none of those will stay scarce. It competes on the part that stays hard.

The work

Each answers a different question, and the question comes first. It determines scope, the databases and classification ranges worked, the date bounds applied, and what the report has to establish.

  • Freedom to operate

    Can this be practised, and where?

    A defined product, process or formulation read against granted in-force claims, and against pending claims that could still issue, in the jurisdictions that matter. Legal status is verified at family level rather than inferred — lapses, revocations, and any term extension or SPC that moves an expiry date. Anything capable of reading on the embodiment is charted limitation by limitation, with the basis for each conclusion set out rather than asserted.

  • Patentability

    Is there anything left to claim?

    A pre-filing search across patent and non-patent literature, run before drafting time is committed. Scoped to the inventive concept rather than to the disclosure as drafted, so the closest art surfaces against novelty and against obviousness while the claims can still be shaped around it. Classification ranges are worked alongside text, because the reference that anticipates rarely uses the applicant's vocabulary.

  • Invalidity and validity

    What did the examiner miss — and will it hold?

    The same discipline from opposite postures. Against an asserted patent, an adversarial search bounded to the earliest effective filing date, directed at anticipation and at obviousness combinations, with the public-availability date of each reference evidenced rather than assumed — the point at which invalidity contentions most often fail. Built to go into an opposition, a petition or a pre-suit assessment, where the window is fixed and does not reopen. For a holder, the same search run before assertion, acquisition or licensing, so the weak point is known in advance rather than discovered in a response.

  • Infringement and evidence of use

    Who is practising the claims?

    Granted claims mapped onto commercial products and processes, evidenced from public technical material — datasheets, regulatory submissions, service manuals, standards contributions, published literature — with each limitation tied to a source a third party can retrieve. Where the claim is drawn to a method, the evidence has to show the step being performed rather than the capacity to perform it.

  • Landscape and technology mapping

    Where is the field going, and who holds it?

    Structured mapping of a technology or therapeutic area: filing velocity, assignee positions normalised across subsidiaries and acquisitions, family-level jurisdictional coverage, classification co-occurrence and white space. Built to inform a decision — a portfolio move, a financing round, an acquisition — which means the analysis is only as broad as that decision requires. A landscape that answers everything answers nothing in time to be useful.

  • Licensing and monetisation support

    Which assets earn, and who is already using them?

    Portfolio analysis directed at revenue rather than at protection: asset mining and ranking to surface the claims with commercial reach, evidence-of-use work identifying who is already practising them, counterpart mapping across jurisdictions, and maintenance-fee analysis on assets costing more than they return. What this produces is the evidential groundwork a licensing programme is built on. Terms, valuation opinions and negotiation remain matters for counsel.

  • Patent brokerage

    Who would buy this, and how does it reach them?

    Representation of holders looking to sell or out-license — often individual inventors and small portfolios with no route to a buyer. The asset is packaged so an acquirer's technical team can assess it quickly: claims read against the market, evidence of use where it exists, family and legal status set out, and the technical case written for someone who has not lived with the invention. Approaches run through a network of IP professionals built over two decades rather than through a listing. Terms, diligence and the transaction itself sit with the parties and their counsel.

  • Claim charts and claim mapping

    Show the read, limitation by limitation.

    Every limitation set against the specific disclosure said to meet it, cited to column and line for granted US patents and to paragraph number for published applications. Charts are prepared to be handed onward — to a client, an opponent, a tribunal — and written so that each row survives being read without the searcher in the room.

  • Sequence and chemical structure searching

    Find what text retrieval cannot reach.

    Nucleotide and amino acid sequence searching, with alignments reported on identity and coverage rather than reduced to a single similarity threshold, and chemical structure, substructure and Markush searching for genus claims that text retrieval cannot reach. Sequence listings are worked to WIPO ST.26. Across life sciences and HiTech this is where a search is won or lost, and it is the part most often approximated.

How a search is run

Most of what separates a useful search report from an expensive one is procedural. These are the standing terms of every engagement.

  • Scope is agreed in writing before anything begins

    Technology, claim or concept, jurisdictions, date bounds, databases to be queried, and what the report has to establish. Search is expensive to redirect once underway; the scoping document is where that cost is avoided.

  • The strategy is disclosed, not concealed

    Search strings, the CPC and IPC ranges worked, every database queried and the date bounds applied are recorded in the report. Where recall was traded against precision, the report says where and why. A search whose method is invisible cannot be defended, extended or relied on — and cannot be told apart from one that was never run.

  • An interim checkpoint before the search completes

    Early findings are put in front of you while scope can still be corrected cheaply. Where the art is pointing somewhere unexpected, that is worth knowing in week one rather than at delivery.

  • Delivery is set against your deadline, not ours

    Opposition windows, petition bars and filing dates do not move. The delivery date is fixed at scoping and worked backwards from yours. Where a firm has a house chart or report format, the deliverable arrives in that format rather than in one you then have to rebuild.

  • Every conclusion cited to the line

    Findings are tied to specific passages, not to documents. The standard is that any row of any chart can be verified by a reader who is hostile to it, without asking a question.

  • Questions after delivery are part of the engagement

    Follow-up, clarification and a reasonable round of extension are included. A report that generates a second invoice every time it is queried is not a finished report.

How the practice works

Sambyal IP is deliberately small and intends to remain so. What follows are choices, not a stage of growth.

  • One engagement at a time

    Work is accepted only when it can have the practice's full attention from scoping through to report, and nothing is run alongside it. That limits how much is taken on, which is the point: the alternative is dividing attention across matters where a missed reference is measured in years of prosecution or in litigation exposure. Capacity is added when the work requires it, not to meet a number.

  • By referral

    There is no outbound programme and no enquiry funnel behind this page. Work arrives through relationships built over two decades and through introductions made within them.

  • Conflicts cleared before scope

    Any new matter is screened against current and prior engagements before technical detail is exchanged. Where a conflict exists we say so and decline. The written screening procedure is available to a conflicts desk on request.

  • Client material held to enterprise standard

    Material arrives here before it is public. It is held in an enterprise cloud environment with encryption in transit and at rest, two-step verification enforced on every account with access, and that access confined to the engagement it belongs to. Retention and return are agreed at scoping rather than assumed. The underlying data processing terms and the handling practice are supplied to an information security desk on request.

  • Nothing published, ever

    Unfiled applications, invention disclosures and draft claims pass through this practice. There are no client names on this page, no case studies and no testimonials, and there will not be. A practice that publishes one client's work has told the next client exactly what to expect.

The foundation

IP search in the life sciences and in HiTech sits on a seam between the science and the claim. A searcher who reads the science but not the claim returns art that is interesting and does not matter. One who reads the claim but not the science walks past the disclosure that does. Work that holds up is done standing on both sides of it.

Behind this practice is twenty years of exactly that work, across US, UK, European and Asian portfolios, and every engagement is reviewed against the standard that work set.

Searching is conducted in professional patent and non-patent literature databases, with sequence and structure work run in the specialist systems those subject matters require.

What this practice is not

Sambyal IP is not a law firm and does not practise law. It provides scientific and technical search, analysis and data services. Nothing it produces is legal advice, a legal opinion or a clearance. The search is delivered to the attorney, who forms the opinion and carries it.

Enquiries

The practice does not solicit work. Introductions made from within the relationships it already holds are considered as capacity allows, and there is no process beyond that. A mutual non-disclosure agreement precedes any technical discussion.
