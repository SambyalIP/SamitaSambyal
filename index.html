Sambyal IP searches, analyzes and charts patents and non-patent literature for Life Sciences and HiTech attorneys — testing an invention, clearing a product, breaking a claim, defending one, proving a use, finding the white space. It works by referral, and takes one engagement at a time.

The position

Retrieval is no longer the hard part. AI has made prior art cheap: any competent tool now returns more of it than a matter can absorb, and the cost of producing results is falling toward zero. What none of it does is decide.

The work that survives that shift is judgment. Scoping a search to the question actually being asked. Recognizing the reference that reads on a claim when nothing in its language says so. Producing an analysis that holds when someone is paid to break it.

This practice is built for that end state rather than adapted to it. It does not compete on volume, turnaround tables or headcount, because none of those will stay scarce. It competes on the part that stays hard.

The work

Each answers a different question, and the question comes first. It determines scope, the databases and classification ranges worked, the date bounds applied, and what the report has to establish.

  • Freedom to operate

    Can this be practiced, and where?

    Granted in-force claims and pending claims that could still issue, in the jurisdictions that matter. Legal status is verified at family level rather than inferred — lapses, revocations, and any term extension or SPC that moves an expiry date. Anything capable of reading on the embodiment is charted limitation by limitation.

  • Patentability

    Is there anything left to claim?

    Scoped to the inventive concept rather than to the disclosure as drafted, so the closest art surfaces while the claims can still be shaped around it. Classification ranges are worked alongside text, because the reference that anticipates rarely uses the applicant's vocabulary.

  • Invalidity and validity

    What did the examiner miss — and will it hold?

    An adversarial search bounded to the earliest effective filing date, directed at anticipation and at obviousness combinations, with the public-availability date of each reference evidenced rather than assumed — the point at which invalidity contentions most often fail. Run in the same discipline for a holder before assertion, acquisition or licensing, so the weak point is known in advance rather than discovered in a response.

  • Infringement and evidence of use

    Who is practicing the claims?

    Granted claims mapped onto commercial products and processes, evidenced from public technical material, with each limitation tied to a source a third party can retrieve. Where the claim is drawn to a method, the evidence has to show the step being performed rather than the capacity to perform it.

  • Landscape and technology mapping

    Where is the field going, and who holds it?

    Filing velocity, assignee positions normalized across subsidiaries and acquisitions, family-level jurisdictional coverage, classification co-occurrence and white space. Built to inform one decision, which means the analysis is only as broad as that decision requires. A landscape that answers everything answers nothing in time to be useful.

  • Licensing and monetization support

    Which assets earn, and who is already using them?

    Asset mining and ranking to surface the claims with commercial reach, evidence-of-use work identifying who is already practicing them, counterpart mapping across jurisdictions, and maintenance-fee analysis on assets costing more than they return. The evidential groundwork a licensing program is built on. Terms, valuation opinions and negotiation remain matters for counsel.

  • Asset packaging and counterparty mapping

    Who would buy this, and what do they need to see?

    Analytical and packaging work for holders weighing a sale or out-license — often individual inventors and small portfolios. The asset is prepared so an acquirer's technical team can assess it quickly: claims read against the market, evidence of use where it exists, family and legal status set out, and the technical case written for someone who has not lived with the invention. Alongside it, a mapped set of plausible counterparties — who is building in the space, who is already practicing the claims, where the asset closes a portfolio gap. Fees are fixed and scoped to that analysis. The approach, the terms, the diligence and the transaction sit with the holder and their counsel.

  • Claim charts and claim mapping

    Show the read, limitation by limitation.

    Every limitation set against the specific disclosure said to meet it, cited to column and line for granted US patents and to paragraph number for published applications. Written so that each row survives being read without the searcher in the room.

  • Sequence and chemical structure searching

    Find what text retrieval cannot reach.

    Nucleotide and amino acid sequence searching, with alignments reported on identity and coverage rather than reduced to a single similarity threshold. Chemical structure, substructure and Markush searching for genus claims text retrieval cannot reach. Sequence listings are worked to WIPO ST.26 — the part most often approximated.

How a search is run

Most of what separates a useful search report from an expensive one is procedural. These are the standing terms of every engagement.

  • Scope is agreed in writing before anything begins

    Technology, claim or concept, jurisdictions, date bounds, databases to be queried, and what the report has to establish — settled before work starts.

  • The strategy is disclosed, not concealed

    Search strings, the CPC and IPC ranges worked, every database queried and the date bounds applied are recorded in the report. Where AI or semantic tools were used, it says which, at which step, and what was checked by hand afterwards. Where recall was traded against precision, it says where and why. A search whose method is invisible cannot be defended, extended or relied on — and cannot be told apart from one that was never run.

  • An interim checkpoint before the search completes

    Early findings go in front of you while scope can still be corrected. Where the art points somewhere unexpected, that is worth knowing in week one rather than at delivery.

  • Delivery is set against your deadline, not ours

    The delivery date is fixed at scoping and worked backwards from yours. Where a firm has a house chart or report format, the deliverable arrives in that format rather than in one you then have to rebuild.

  • Every conclusion cited to the line

    Findings are tied to specific passages, not to documents. The standard is that any row of any chart can be verified by a reader who is hostile to it, without asking a question.

  • Questions after delivery are part of the engagement

    Follow-up, clarification and a reasonable round of extension are included. A report that generates a second invoice every time it is queried is not a finished report.

How the practice works

Sambyal IP is deliberately small and intends to remain so. What follows are choices, not a stage of growth.

  • One engagement at a time

    Work is accepted only when it can have the practice's full attention from scoping through to report, and nothing runs alongside it. That limits how much is taken on, which is the point. Capacity is added when the work requires it, not to meet a number.

  • By referral

    There is no outbound program and no inquiry funnel behind this page. Work arrives through introduction.

  • Conflicts cleared before scope

    Any new matter is screened against current and prior engagements before technical detail is exchanged. Where a conflict exists we say so and decline. The written screening procedure is available to a conflicts desk on request.

  • Client material held to enterprise standard

    Material arrives here before it is public. It is held in an enterprise cloud environment with encryption in transit and at rest, two-step verification on every account with access, and that access confined to the engagement it belongs to. Nothing that arrives here enters a public AI service or trains a model; where AI-assisted tooling is used, it runs inside that environment. Retention and return are agreed at scoping. The data processing terms and the handling practice go to an information security desk on request.

  • Nothing published, ever

    Unfiled applications, invention disclosures and draft claims pass through this practice. There are no client names on this page, no case studies and no testimonials, and there will not be. A practice that publishes one client's work has told the next client exactly what to expect.

The standard

Every reference has to be read twice: for what it discloses, and for what it claims. Read only for the technology, a search returns art that is interesting and beside the point. Read only for the claim, it walks past the disclosure that decides the matter. Both readings are run on every reference, on the same pass.

Subject matter spans electronics and software, mechanical, pharmaceutical, biomedical and agrichemical, clinical and molecular diagnostics.

Patent databases are the starting point, not the boundary. Scientific and technical literature, conference proceedings and abstracts, theses, preprints, clinical trial registries, regulatory submissions, standards contributions, grant records, technical manuals and defensive publications are worked as sources in their own right, with the public-availability date of anything relied on evidenced rather than assumed. The reference that decides a matter is often not a patent, and is rarely indexed where a patent search would find it. Sequence and structure work runs in the specialist systems those subject matters require.

The search goes to the attorney, in the attorney's format and on the attorney's deadline. The opinion stays with the attorney.

What this practice is not

Sambyal IP is not a law firm and does not practice law. It provides scientific and technical search, analysis and data services on its own account. It does not act as agent for any party, holds no authority to bind anyone, and is not remunerated by reference to the value of any transaction. Nothing it produces is legal advice, a legal opinion, a clearance or a valuation. Terms, diligence and negotiation sit with the parties and their counsel, and the search is delivered to the attorney, who forms the opinion and carries it.

Inquiries

The practice does not solicit work. Introductions are considered as capacity allows, and there is no process beyond that. A mutual non-disclosure agreement precedes any technical discussion.
